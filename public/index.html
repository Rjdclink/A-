    CUSTOMER RECOVERY

    Prioritize, follow up, measure

    Rank real customer signals by intent and recorded opportunity value. Messages use those facts; amounts are opportunities, not guaranteed recoveries.

    Follow-up sequence and experiment

    Three messages maximum. Each channel requires recorded consent. Purchases, replies and opt-outs stop follow-ups. Holdouts receive no recovery messages. Use a new experiment name when changing a running sequence.

    Add a customer recovery signal
    Record a purchase, reply or opt-out

    Commerce purchases and signed Shopify orders also stop sequences automatically when their customer IDs match.

    Recovery learning

    Randomized holdout comparison, separated by currency. Only customers with a complete observation window count. Negative lift stays visible.

    Message activity

    Accepted means the provider accepted a message, not confirmed delivery. Unknown delivery requires provider review and never automatically retries.

    Failed-payment recovery

    Stripe owns charge retries and payment-update emails. A+ tracks unpaid invoices, next retry time and paid status without duplicating charges.

    Configure Stripe Smart Retries ↗
    Connect incoming signals

    Customer contacts: POST /api/recovery/engine/contacts. Events: POST /api/recovery/engine/signals. Use the same customer ID for cart, checkout and purchase. Stripe signed webhook: /api/recovery/webhooks/stripe. Twilio signed inbound webhook: /api/recovery/webhooks/twilio. Provider keys and webhook secrets go in Settings → Service setup.
